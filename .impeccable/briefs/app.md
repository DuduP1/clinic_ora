# Surface brief – authenticated app (all screens under src/app/(app) + login)

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led (Operate surface; launcher unavailable, no comps).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The CRM is the clinic's reception book, not a SaaS dashboard. It refuses the category default of white cards, four KPI tiles and a stock accent; the day's schedule is the first thing staff see.

OWN-WORLD: Espresso and champagne gold from the lotus logo. Deep espresso shell (sidebar) with ivory text and a gold active marker; porcelain-white working ground (not cream); hairline taupe rules; muted jewel status set (sapphire / honey / sage / stone / garnet). Bellefair for titles and the day's date; Heebo for every label, control and datum.

STORY: Staff open the app and immediately know who is coming in today and what is waiting; they find a customer by phone from anywhere and act in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard = greeting + date set in Bellefair; under it one quiet status strip (new · in progress · without owner [garnet when >0] · appointments today) replacing the tile grid; the main area is "היום ביומן", a vertical time rail 08:00–20:00 with gold hairline hour ticks, appointments placed by time, a gold "now" line; beside it the open-tickets-by-owner table. Primary actions (+ פנייה / + תור / + לקוחה) top-left, espresso primary.

FORM: Reception day-book / timetable rhythm (own grounded list #1; user-pinned direction "בוטיק זהב"). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
